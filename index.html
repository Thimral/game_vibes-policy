Game Vibes

Privacy policy · last updated 20 November 2026

Game Vibes is a set of party games played on one device. It has no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and the people who make it receive no information about you or the people you play with.

What stays on your device

The players' names you type, and your settings (age level, era, light or dark look), are saved on your device so they are there next time, along with a list of which cards have been shown (so a name or question does not come back too soon). They are never sent to us or anyone else. Remove names in Team Maker (one at a time, or Clear all), or uninstall the app to remove everything.

The TV display

In a Party Session you can choose to show the scoreboard on another screen on the same network. Only while it is on, the device shares the game's title, public clues, timers, team or player names and scores through a private link that only works on your own network. It is not sent over the internet, and it stops when you turn it off or leave the session. Besides this, the app uses the network only to talk to Google Play about the purchase described below.

Purchases

Game Vibes is free to play. One optional, one-time purchase, made through Google Play, unlocks more games, the 18+ cards and every era. Google Play takes the payment under its own terms and privacy policy: we never see your payment details or your name. The app only learns whether this Google account has bought the unlock, keeps that answer on your device, and asks Google Play again each time it starts, so a refund or a new phone is handled. There is no subscription, and nothing is bought without your confirmation in Google Play's own screen.

What we collect

Nothing. Game Vibes does not collect, store on a server, sell or share personal data, and contains no third-party tracking or advertising code. Purchases are processed by Google Play, not by us.

Children

Game Vibes collects no data from anyone, including children. The Family setting is the default; 13+ and 18+ settings add older topics and can be changed only in Settings.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published here with a new date.
